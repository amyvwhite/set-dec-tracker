# SET DEC TRACKING APP (MASTER TEMPLATE)

## How to use this template for a New Show:
1. Copy this entire folder into your new show's folder (or rename the copy, e.g. "SHOW NAME - SET DEC APP").
2. Double-click "Run App.bat" to launch the app.
3. In the App, click SETTINGS (top right):
   - Paste your new Google Sheet Tracker URL.
   - Enter your Expense Company / Location / Account codes for this show.
   - Click "SAVE CHEAT SHEET & SETTINGS".
4. Drop your receipts into the "Input" folder and click "PROCESS CREDIT CARDS"!
